<p>Before your initial consultation with a law firm concerning a <a href="/personal-injury-overview/">personal injury accident</a>, you should make a list of questions to ask the attorney to make sure your attorney is fully qualified to handle your individual case.</p>
<h2><strong>The attorney you select can make a substantial difference in the eventual outcome of your case.</strong></h2>
<p>Attorneys are normally reluctant to take over a case from an attorney who has already initiated a case in court because it is frequently very difficult to fix mistakes other attorneys have made after a case has been filed in court. A case can quickly become tainted by an inexperienced lawyer who makes decisions that can be difficult to undo at a later date. It is critical to hire the right attorney for your particular case.</p>
<p><strong>The following is a list of questions for your lawyer:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>What percentage of your law practice is focused on personal injury cases?</li>
  <li>How many personal injury cases have you taken to trial and what were the results?</li>
  <li>Do other law firms refer personal injury cases to you?</li>
  <li>How much do you think my case is worth?</li>
  <li>How long do you expect it will take to resolve my case?</li>
  <li>Do cases like my case normally settle out of court?</li>
  <li>Do you foresee any issues or difficulties with my case?</li>
  <li>Will you be the attorney that is actually handling my case?</li>
  <li>Will you be referring my case to a different law firm?</li>
  <li>Will other attorneys from your office be working on my case?</li>
  <li>How often will I receive updates about my case?</li>
  <li>Will I be able to speak directly with the attorney working on my case if I have questions?</li>
  <li>Do you have the necessary time to devote to my case?</li>
  <li>Do you have the necessary staffing and financial resources to take on my case?</li>
  <li>Has your license ever been suspended or revoked? If so, why?</li>
  <li>What is your reputation in the legal community?</li>
  <li>What is your fee arrangement?</li>
</ul>
