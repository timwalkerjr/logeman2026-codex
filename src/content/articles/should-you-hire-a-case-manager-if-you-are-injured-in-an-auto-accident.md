<p>Case management services are a covered no-fault insurance benefit if you or a family member is seriously injured in an <a href="/motor-vehicle-accidents-overview/car-accident-attorney-ann-arbor-mi/">auto accident</a>. Typically, a case manager is utilized when the injured party suffers a brain injury, has severe or complex medical issues to manage, or is elderly or a young accident victim. An injured person has the right to hire their own case manager and is not required to utilize the insurance company's case manager.</p>

<h2><strong>It is recommended that an accident victim hire their own independent case manager.</strong></h2>
<p>Case managers will advocate on your behalf and assist in managing your medical needs including the following services:</p>

<h3><strong>Communicate with the insurance company</strong></h3>
<p>A case manager should be able to effectively communicate with the insurance adjuster, including providing the necessary prescriptions to expedite processing of your <a href="/motor-vehicle-accidents-overview/no-fault-claim-attorney-ann-arbor-mi/">no-fault insurance claims</a>.</p>

<h3><strong>Educate you and your family about your diagnosis</strong></h3>
<p>Your case manager helps you understand your diagnosis so you can make informed decisions.</p>

<h3><strong>Maintain communication</strong></h3>
<p>To ensure that your medical doctors, treatment team, and the adjuster are on the same page.</p>

<h3><strong>Help you transition to different care levels</strong></h3>
<p>As you move from different levels of care depending on your ongoing treatment, your case manager helps you understand and prepare for coming changes.</p>

<h3><strong>Coordinate paperwork and documentation</strong></h3>
<p>Paperwork can be overwhelming. Your case manager coordinates paperwork with providers and insurance companies, ensuring timely communications, correctly filled-out forms, and organized record-keeping.</p>

<h3><strong>Assist with scheduling your appointments</strong></h3>
<p>Your case manager reduces the stress of scheduling appointments with multiple medical and rehabilitation providers.</p>

<h3><strong>Schedule transportation and care services</strong></h3>
<p>A case manager can schedule and coordinate necessary medical transportation services.</p>

<h3><strong>Accompany you to medical appointments</strong></h3>
<p>Your case manager is there to support you, advocate for you, and provide explanation of clinical plans of care and doctor recommendations.</p>

<h3><strong>Manage treatment needs</strong></h3>
<p>Assist with purchasing available medical equipment, planning home modifications, and purchasing a modified van if necessary.</p>
