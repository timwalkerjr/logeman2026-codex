<p>Under the new law, as of July 2, 2020, senior citizens will have the option to opt out from no-fault allowable expense PIP benefits if they meet the following conditions:</p>
<ol>
  <li>The person is covered under Part A and B of Medicare;</li>
  <li>The person's spouse and any relative residing in the household has Medicare or a qualified health coverage, or has no-fault PIP coverage under a separate policy.</li>
</ol>
