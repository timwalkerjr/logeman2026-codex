<p>If you were involved in a <a href="/practice-area/car-accidents/">car accident in Michigan</a> and no one was injured, you probably do not need to consult with an attorney. If you purchased collision coverage, your insurer should voluntarily pay for your vehicle damage. If you did not have collision coverage and were not at fault, you would be entitled to up to $3,000 in collision coverage under the Mini Tort law.</p>
<p><strong>Michigan is a no-fault state, and this generally means that your auto insurance company should cover your <a href="/articles/how-to-obtain-compensation-for-a-michigan-car-accident-involving-personal-injury">medical expenses</a> and other related losses regardless of who was to blame in the accident if you purchased medical coverage and were injured.</strong></p>
<p>However, your injuries may exceed the amount covered by your car insurance policy, or your insurance company may determine that certain treatment is not necessary. If this is the case, you may need a lawyer to ensure you receive the maximum compensation for your injuries and that your insurer is paying you all benefits you are entitled to under the law.</p>
<p>You may also need an attorney if you choose to pursue a <a href="/practice-area/uninsured-underinsured-motorist-accidents/">personal injury claim</a> against the other driver involved in the accident. Proving that the other driver caused the accident is not always easy, as they will typically attempt to place the blame on you instead. An experienced car accident lawyer knows how to gather and present the evidence that proves the other driver is to blame for the accident.</p>
<p><strong>You should not wait to consult with an attorney because critical evidence may be destroyed if you wait too long.</strong></p>
<h2><strong>How a Car Accident Lawyer Will Handle Your Claim in Michigan</strong></h2>
<p>The long-term physical, emotional, and financial impacts of a car accident may not be immediately apparent. And they may be difficult to prove. That's why you need an experienced car accident lawyer fighting for you.</p>
<p>Whether you are attempting to recover compensation from your insurance company or from the driver at fault in your accident, a car accident lawyer can help. The attorney will handle every aspect of your claim, including negotiating with your insurance company to ensure you receive financial compensation for:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Current and future medical bills</li>
  <li>Property damage and loss</li>
  <li>Pain and suffering damages</li>
  <li>Lost wages</li>
  <li>Loss of consortium damages</li>
</ul>
