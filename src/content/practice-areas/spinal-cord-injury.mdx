<p>A spinal cord injury can change everything in an instant. Whether caused by a car accident, workplace incident, fall, or medical negligence, these catastrophic injuries often result in permanent paralysis, requiring lifelong medical care, adaptive equipment, and personal assistance.</p>
<p>At Logeman & Iafrate, P.C., our Ann Arbor spinal cord injury attorneys understand the enormous physical, emotional, and financial challenges facing spinal cord injury victims and their families. We fight to secure the maximum compensation needed to address lifetime care requirements.</p>

<h2>Types of Spinal Cord Injuries</h2>
<p>Spinal cord injuries vary in severity and location, affecting different body functions:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Complete injuries</strong> result in total loss of motor function and sensation below the injury site</li>
  <li><strong>Incomplete injuries</strong> allow some function or sensation to remain</li>
  <li><strong>Quadriplegia (tetraplegia)</strong> affects all four limbs when the cervical spine is injured</li>
  <li><strong>Paraplegia</strong> affects the lower body when the thoracic or lumbar spine is injured</li>
</ul>

<h2>Compensation for Spinal Cord Injury Victims</h2>
<p>Spinal cord injury cases require comprehensive evaluation of lifetime costs, including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Immediate medical expenses including emergency care and surgery</li>
  <li>Ongoing rehabilitation and physical therapy</li>
  <li>Adaptive equipment, wheelchairs, and mobility devices</li>
  <li>Home modifications for accessibility</li>
  <li>24-hour attendant care or nursing assistance</li>
  <li>Lost wages and diminished earning capacity</li>
  <li>Pain, suffering, and loss of enjoyment of life</li>
</ul>
<p>Our attorneys work with life care planners, vocational experts, and medical specialists to document the full extent of damages and fight for complete compensation.</p>
