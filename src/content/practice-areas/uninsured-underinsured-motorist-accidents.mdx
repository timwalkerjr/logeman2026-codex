<h2>What Happens In An Accident With An Uninsured Driver?</h2>
<p>If you are <a href="/motor-vehicle-accidents-overview/">injured by a driver</a> who lacks insurance, the path to compensation can be complex. Without uninsured motorist coverage on your own policy, your options for recovery may be severely limited. This coverage is an additional feature of your auto insurance policy that provides protection when the at-fault driver has no insurance.</p>

<h2>The Role Of Underinsured Motorist Coverage</h2>
<p>Even if the at-fault driver has some level of insurance, it may not be enough to cover the full extent of your damages. Underinsured motorist coverage comes into play here, allowing you to claim the difference from your own policy. For example, if your total damages amount to $100,000 and the other driver's insurance covers only $50,000, your underinsured motorist coverage can help cover the remaining $50,000.</p>

<h2>Essential Steps To Take At The Accident Scene And Throughout The Process</h2>
<p><strong>At the scene:</strong> Ensure everyone's safety and call emergency services. Exchange information with the other driver and take photos of the scene if possible.</p>
<p><strong>Medical attention:</strong> Seek immediate medical care, even if you feel fine. Some injuries may not be immediately apparent.</p>
<p><strong>Report the accident:</strong> Notify your insurance company about the accident. Be factual about the details, but avoid admitting fault.</p>
