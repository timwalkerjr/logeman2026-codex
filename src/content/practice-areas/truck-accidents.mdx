<h2>Truck Accidents Are Complex, You Need An Experienced Attorney To Help</h2>
<p>If you or a loved one has been involved in a truck accident, the path to recovery can be daunting. Truck accidents often result in major or <a href="/catastrophic-injuries/">catastrophic injuries</a> due to the massive size and weight of these vehicles. At Logeman & Iafrate, P.C., we know how to dissect these complexities and craft a solid legal strategy to protect your rights and ensure you receive the compensation you deserve for medical care, lost wages and more.</p>
<p>Truck accidents can involve a variety of unique issues:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Stopping distance:</strong> Trucks require significantly more room to stop than other vehicles.</li>
  <li><strong>Maintenance requirements:</strong> Trucking companies must adhere to strict maintenance schedules.</li>
  <li><strong>Driver fatigue:</strong> Federal regulations limit driving hours to prevent fatigue.</li>
  <li><strong>Load balancing:</strong> Improperly loaded or secured cargo can cause accidents.</li>
  <li><strong>Distracted driving:</strong> Texting, eating or focusing on anything but the road can lead to serious accidents.</li>
  <li><strong>Driving under the influence:</strong> Trucking is a stressful job, and it is not uncommon to see accidents caused by drug and alcohol use.</li>
</ul>
