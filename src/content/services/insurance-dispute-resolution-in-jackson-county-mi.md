<h2>Common Insurance Company Tactics in Jackson County</h2>

<p>Insurance adjusters and their lawyers use predictable strategies to deny or minimize your claim:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Blaming pre-existing conditions.</strong> They argue your current medical needs stem from old injuries, not the recent accident.</li>
  <li><strong>Disputing medical necessity.</strong> Insurers claim your treatment is excessive, experimental, or not related to covered injuries.</li>
  <li><strong>Misinterpreting policy language.</strong> They exploit ambiguous terms or claim exclusions apply when coverage is actually clear.</li>
  <li><strong>Delaying investigations.</strong> The longer they drag out the process, the more likely you are to settle for less or abandon the claim entirely.</li>
  <li><strong>Low-ball settlement offers.</strong> Initial offers rarely reflect the full value of your claim, hoping you'll accept out of financial desperation.</li>
</ul>

<p>These tactics work on unrepresented claimants. They don't work when experienced trial attorneys are involved.</p>

<p>We hold your insurance company to its contractual obligations, pursuing full compensation under your UM/UIM coverage when the at-fault party can't fully pay for your losses.</p>
