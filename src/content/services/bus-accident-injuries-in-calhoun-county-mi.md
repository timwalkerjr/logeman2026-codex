<h2>Why Bus Accidents Cause Severe Injuries</h2>

<p>Unlike passenger cars, most buses lack basic safety features that protect occupants during crashes. Passengers typically aren't wearing seatbelts. There are no airbags. The high center of gravity makes rollovers more likely. When a bus collides with another vehicle, strikes a pedestrian, or crashes due to driver error, the results are often devastating.</p>

<p>Common causes of Calhoun County bus accidents include:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Driver negligence:</strong> Distracted driving, fatigue, speeding, or failure to follow traffic laws</li>
  <li><strong>Inadequate maintenance:</strong> Brake failures, tire blowouts, or mechanical defects</li>
  <li><strong>Poor training:</strong> Inexperienced drivers who can't safely operate large commercial vehicles</li>
  <li><strong>Road conditions:</strong> Hazards that bus operators should anticipate and avoid</li>
  <li><strong>Third-party negligence:</strong> Other motorists who cause crashes involving buses</li>
</ul>

<h2>Michigan Law and Bus Accident Claims</h2>

<p>Bus accident claims in Calhoun County involve complex legal issues that require experienced attorneys who understand Michigan's unique insurance and liability laws.</p>

<p><strong>No-Fault Benefits:</strong> If you were a passenger on a bus or injured while in another vehicle, you're entitled to Personal Injury Protection (PIP) benefits from your own auto insurance or the bus company's policy. These benefits cover medical expenses, wage loss, and other costs regardless of who caused the accident.</p>

<p><strong>Third-Party Liability Claims:</strong> You can sue the at-fault party if your injuries meet Michigan's threshold for "serious impairment of body function." This allows recovery of pain and suffering, excess medical expenses, and other damages beyond no-fault benefits.</p>

<p><strong>Governmental Immunity:</strong> Claims against public transit authorities involve special notice requirements and limitations. You typically must file a notice of claim within 120 days of the accident, and damage caps may apply. Missing these deadlines can destroy your case.</p>

<p><strong>Federal Regulations:</strong> Commercial bus companies must comply with Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations governing driver qualifications, hours of service, vehicle maintenance, and safety protocols. Violations of these regulations can strengthen your claim.</p>
