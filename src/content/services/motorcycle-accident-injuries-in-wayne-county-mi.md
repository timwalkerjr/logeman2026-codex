<h2><strong>Common Causes of Motorcycle Accidents in Wayne County</strong></h2>
<p>Most motorcycle accidents are caused by other drivers who fail to see motorcycles or violate riders' rights on the road. We represent Wayne County riders injured in crashes involving:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Left-turn accidents.</strong> The most common motorcycle crash—drivers turning left fail to see oncoming motorcycles and turn directly into their path.</li>
  <li><strong>Lane-change accidents.</strong> Drivers who change lanes without checking blind spots strike motorcyclists riding alongside their vehicles.</li>
  <li><strong>Rear-end collisions.</strong> Drivers who tailgate motorcycles or fail to brake in time cause devastating rear-end crashes.</li>
  <li><strong>Distracted driving.</strong> Texting, eating, or adjusting controls takes drivers' attention off motorcyclists sharing the road.</li>
  <li><strong>Impaired driving.</strong> Drivers under the influence of alcohol or drugs pose extreme dangers to motorcyclists.</li>
  <li><strong>Unsafe passing.</strong> Drivers who pass motorcycles too closely or cut back into the lane too quickly cause riders to lose control.</li>
  <li><strong>Dooring accidents.</strong> Drivers or passengers who open doors into the path of oncoming motorcyclists.</li>
</ul>
<p>None of these crashes are the motorcyclist's fault. Yet insurance companies often try to blame riders anyway. Our attorneys know how to counter these defenses and prove the at-fault driver's negligence.</p>

<h2><strong>Michigan's Motorcycle Insurance Requirements</strong></h2>
<p>Michigan's no-fault insurance system treats motorcycles differently than cars. While car owners are required to purchase unlimited PIP benefits, motorcycle riders are not required to carry PIP coverage—though many choose to do so for protection. If you carry motorcycle PIP, you may recover limited benefits through your own policy. Regardless of your PIP coverage, if your injuries meet Michigan's serious impairment threshold, you can pursue a third-party claim against the at-fault driver for pain and suffering, excess economic damages, and other losses.</p>
<p>This complex insurance landscape makes experienced legal representation essential. Our attorneys understand the nuances of motorcycle insurance in Michigan and work to identify every available source of compensation.</p>

<h2><strong>Compensation Available After a Wayne County Motorcycle Accident</strong></h2>
<p>Michigan law allows injured motorcyclists to recover significant compensation when the at-fault driver was negligent. Available damages include:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Pain and suffering.</strong> Compensation for physical pain and emotional distress caused by your injuries.</li>
  <li><strong>Medical expenses.</strong> All current and future medical costs, including surgeries, rehabilitation, medications, and assistive devices.</li>

  <li><strong>Excess economic damages.</strong> Medical expenses and wage losses beyond what insurance coverage provides.</li>
</ul>
<p>Successfully recovering these damages requires proving the at-fault driver's negligence and the full extent of your injuries. Our attorneys build comprehensive cases that account for both current losses and future needs.</p>
